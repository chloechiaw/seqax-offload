  hold work-per-chip fixed, add chips. perfect = step time unchanged.

   chips  batch  step ms   tokens/sec  vs half the chips
       2      8      312       26,214                  -
       4     16      250       65,536              2.50x
       8     32      250      131,072              2.00x

       2     16      562       29,127                  -
       4     32      438       74,898              2.57x
       8     64      438      149,797              2.00x

  -> 4 to 8 chips is EXACTLY 2.00x, twice. inside a slice it scales perfectly.
